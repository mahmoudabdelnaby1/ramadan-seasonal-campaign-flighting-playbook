# Retail Ecommerce Ramadan

## Technical Reference & Operating Handbook: Ramadan & Seasonal Retail Campaign Flighting Playbook
Comprehensive documentation and standard operating procedures for **Ramadan & Seasonal Retail Campaign Flighting Playbook** within the **media-buying** domain.

### Executive Overview
Create an authoritative Ramadan and Eid marketing campaign flighting framework: 1) 4-phase campaign blueprint (Pre-Ramadan warming, Mid-Ramadan engagement, Late-Ramadan gifting rush, Post-Eid clearance), 2) Mathematical budget allocation model (15% - 25% - 45% - 15%), 3) Dayparting bid scheduling rules for nighttime shopping spikes, 4) Real-world creative messaging matrix per phase.

## Core Operational Principles

1. **Deterministic & Auditable Workflows:** Every parameter, threshold, and scoring rule must be grounded in observable telemetry rather than intuition.
2. **Regional Compliance & Localization:** Full adherence to GCC (Saudi GAMR, ZATCA, SAMA) and Egypt regulatory frameworks for e-commerce and digital communications.
3. **Fail-Safe Operational Guardrails:** Built-in safeguards against runaway ad spend, deliverability burn, or unmonitored lead SLA breaches.
