## 📌 Problem Statement & Target Persona

### Target Persona
**BOTH** operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

### Core Problem
Create an authoritative Ramadan and Eid marketing campaign flighting framework: 1) 4-phase campaign blueprint (Pre-Ramadan warming, Mid-Ramadan engagement, Late-Ramadan gifting rush, Post-Eid clearance), 2) illustrative budget allocation example (15% - 25% - 45% - 15%) to be replaced with account-specific pacing, 3) Dayparting bid scheduling rules for nighttime shopping spikes, 4) Real-world creative messaging matrix per phase.

## ⚠️ Known Technical & Domain Constraints

- **Iftar & Suhoor Dayparting:** Ad impressions shift drastically towards 10 PM - 3 AM; daytime bids require tactical deprioritization.
- **Phase-Based Budget Allocation:** Campaign spend splits into 4 distinct phases (Pre-Ramadan Stocking, Early Fasting, Mid-Month Peak, Eid Gifting).
- **Courier Blackout Buffers:** Saudi and Gulf couriers halt last-mile delivery 4 to 6 days before Eid al-Fitr, forcing earlier shipping cutoffs.
