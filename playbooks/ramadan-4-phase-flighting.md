# Ramadan 4-Phase Retail Campaign Flighting Playbook

## Phase 1: Pre-Ramadan Preparation & Gifting Teaser (Weeks -2 to 0)
- **Objective:** Audience building, wishlist saving, catalog engagement.
- **Budget Share:** 15% of total seasonal spend.
- **Key Message:** استعد للشهر الفضيل مع تشكيلاتنا الحصرية.

## Phase 2: Ramadan Weeks 1-2 (Family, Food & Home Essentials)
- **Objective:** Immediate conversion for home and grocery goods.
- **Budget Share:** 30% of total spend.

## Phase 3: Ramadan Weeks 3-4 (Eid Gifting & Luxury Rush)
- **Objective:** Maximum conversion volume for fashion, perfumes, jewelry. Peak delivery cutoff deadlines.
- **Budget Share:** 45% of total spend.

## Phase 4: Post-Eid Clearance (Eid Week)
- **Objective:** Clearance sales, digital vouchers.
- **Budget Share:** 10% of total spend.
